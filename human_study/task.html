Match the sound

We used an AI model to re-generate real sound recordings. This study has two short parts:

  • Part 1 (16 quick rounds): you hear two clips, A and B — one is a real recording, the other is the AI-generated version of it. Pick the one you think is real.
  • Part 2 (26 rounds): you hear one generated sound and 6 real recordings of the same kind of sound. Exactly one of the 6 is the recording it was generated from — it often keeps the same rhythm, pitch, or overall shape. Pick that one.
  • Use headphones in a quiet room if you can. You can replay everything as often as you like.
  • 42 rounds, about 12 minutes in total. Attention checks: a few rounds are deliberately easy checks that anyone listening will get right. Everyone who finishes is paid, but responses that fail the checks are excluded from the research data — please listen before answering.
